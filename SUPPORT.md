# Support

**Retired — September 2026.** df-syncer-windows is unmaintained and receives no
further maintenance, features, bug fixes, or security updates. No version is
supported. Issues, feature requests, pull requests, and support or vulnerability
reports are no longer accepted or triaged. There is no response-time commitment.

The [README](README.md), [development notes](CONTRIBUTING.md), and
[security documentation](SECURITY.md) remain as historical reference. Existing
releases are unsupported historical artifacts, not a recommendation to install.

Independent forks are welcome under the existing [MIT License](LICENSE), including
its copyright and license notice requirements. Each fork is independently
maintained and sets its own support policy; no successor is designated here.
